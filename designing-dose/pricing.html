How does the "Pause" subscription feature work?
Billing cycles operate on a 31-day period. If you use the service for 20 days and finish your current sprint, you can pause your membership. The remaining 11 days stay banked for whenever you're ready to restart.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment?
No long-term contracts. Monthly plans are month-to-month and can be canceled anytime with a single click in your billing portal before the next renewal.
What if I only need a single specific website build?
Toggle the "Fixed Projects" button above. We provide comprehensive fixed-fee milestone agreements (50% upfront / 50% upon successful delivery) with guaranteed delivery dates.
What if I'm not satisfied with a design deliverable?
We offer unlimited revisions until you are 100% delighted. Because we share Figma files early and often, we pivot quickly to align with your expectations.
