Client Lumen Organics Ltd.
Services Brand, UI/UX, Shopify Plus
Timeline 6 Weeks (Sprint 1–3)
Year 2026
The Challenge

Transforming clinical efficacy into pure luxury

Lumen formulated dermatological-grade botanicals that outperformed leading cosmetic lab brands, yet their digital presence resembled an outdated clinical supplier. Customer acquisition costs were climbing, and bounce rates on mobile reached a staggering 58%.

Designing Dose was brought in to prescribe an end-to-end overhaul: establishing a tranquil, tactile visual identity and translating it into a bespoke headless Shopify architecture with smooth 60fps micro-interactions.
