Who We Are

A design studio for ambitious teams

We are a compact, senior-only collective of brand architects, interface designers, and creative engineers. No account managers, no junior handoffs — just direct collaboration and unfiltered craft.

Talk with founders
Our Philosophy

Design shouldn't be a marathon; it should be a potent dose

Founded in 2021, Designing Dose emerged as an antidote to bloated legacy agency bureaucracy. Traditional agencies drown projects in endless slide decks, middle management, and months of passive research before delivering generic Figma screens.

We invented the Dosage Methodology: hyper-focused weekly cycles where strategy, aesthetic distinction, and working code happen in tight synchronization. You test real interfaces in days, not quarters.
